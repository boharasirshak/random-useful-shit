# Image credits

- `../brands/chick-fil-a.png`: Chick-fil-A logo, public domain (text logo), via Wikimedia Commons.
- `../brands/panda-express.png`: Panda Express logo, via English Wikipedia (fair use). Used only to label the restaurant.

- `chick-fil-a/`: two Chick-fil-A menu photos from chick-fil-a.com (Oct 2026) for items the catalog has no photo for; the rest moved to `catalog/`.
- `panda-express/`: Panda Express menu photos from pandaexpress.com (Oct 2026), saved from the browser.

- `catalog/`: generated by `packages/food-data/export.mjs`. Chain photos (`cfa-…`, `mcd-…`, `chipotle-…`) are from each chain's own menu site; store product photos (`store-<barcode>`) are from Open Food Facts contributors, CC BY-SA 3.0 (https://world.openfoodfacts.org); the USDA staples (`home-…`) have no photos.

Photos belong to their restaurants and to the Open Food Facts contributors. Only the ones `src/tools/calories/data/menu.ts` points at are bundled into the app.
Items without a photo (e.g. Eggplant Tofu, Quinoa Fried Rice) show an icon instead.
